PatchManager’s user-facing reports are operational evidence maps, not generic dashboards. The device report starts with a compact verdict rail, then a provider/domain health topology beside a deterministic exception queue, followed by the package verification ledger and progressively disclosed audit proof. The fleet report uses the same grammar at estate scale: an estate root connects to functional risk-filter nodes, then the prioritized host ledger begins within the first viewport.

The visual world remains charcoal ink on warm ivory paper with sage for verified completion, amber for review/deferred/reboot states, red only for confirmed failure or exposure, and blue for navigation/action. Components are flat ledger surfaces, fine rules, small radii, explicit state dots, tabular numerals, and authored connector geometry. Avoid large heroes, interchangeable metric cards, decorative charts, gradients, glass, and scroll-gated content.

Search, filters, sorting, links, print, no-JavaScript completeness, offline operation, and existing schemas/exports remain unchanged. Mobile tables become readable evidence cards without page overflow.
